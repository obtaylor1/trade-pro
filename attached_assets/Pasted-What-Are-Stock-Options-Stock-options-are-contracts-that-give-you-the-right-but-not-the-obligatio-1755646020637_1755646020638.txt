What Are Stock Options?

Stock options are contracts that give you the right — but not the obligation — to buy or sell a stock at a specific price (called the strike price) before a certain date (the expiration date).

There are two types:

Call Options → Right to buy a stock at the strike price.

Put Options → Right to sell a stock at the strike price.

💰 Buying a Call Option (Betting the Stock Will Go Up)

Suppose Apple stock (AAPL) trades at $150.

You buy a call option with a strike price of $160, expiring in 1 month.

Premium (cost of the option) = $3 per share = $300 total (since 1 contract = 100 shares).

👉 Two outcomes:

If AAPL goes to $175 → You can buy at $160 and sell at $175.

Profit = ($175 – $160 – $3) × 100 = $1,200.

If AAPL stays below $160 → Option expires worthless.

Loss = $300 (the premium you paid).

📉 Buying a Put Option (Betting the Stock Will Go Down)

Same Apple example, at $150.

You buy a put option with a strike of $140, expiring in 1 month.

Premium = $2 per share = $200 total.

👉 Two outcomes:

If AAPL falls to $120 → You can sell at $140 while market is $120.

Profit = ($140 – $120 – $2) × 100 = $1,800.

If AAPL stays above $140 → Option expires worthless.

Loss = $200.

⚖️ Key Features When Purchasing Stock Options

Premium: The price you pay upfront. This is the maximum you can lose as a buyer.

Strike Price: The stock price at which you can buy (call) or sell (put).

Expiration Date: Options don’t last forever — they lose value as expiration nears (time decay).

Leverage: You control 100 shares of stock for a fraction of the cost. This means big profits or big losses quickly.

Risk/Reward: Buying options = limited risk (premium) but potentially large rewards.

✅ Why People Buy Options

Speculation: To bet on a stock moving up or down without owning it.

Leverage: To control more shares with less money.

Hedging: To protect a portfolio (example: buying puts as insurance against a stock market crash).

📌 Simple Analogy (6th Grade Style):
Buying a stock option is like buying a movie ticket 🎟️.

You pay for the ticket (premium).

If you use it before the showtime (expiration), you see the movie (buy/sell at strike price).

If you don’t, the ticket expires worthless.
